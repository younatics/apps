Pawday (댕오늘) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Pawday is a map of dog-friendly places in Korea with a daily walk index. It has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it keeps your precise location on your device.

Data we collect

Anonymous activity counters. When you open a place, add a favorite or vote 👍/👎, the app adds a count to a public iCloud (CloudKit) table: the place id, the kind of action, the time, which screen it was on and in which position, and your coarse settings context (dog size, transport, weekend, weather). No user id, no device id, no location, no text. These counts power "Trending now". Once a day the app also measures, on the device, how well its picks match your favorites and sends only the result to the same table: two percentages, the number of hidden favorites, your coarse settings context and the build number — no place, no identifier. Apple classifies this as "Product Interaction", not linked to you.

Coarse location for nearby search. To show dog-friendly cafés and restaurants around you, the app sends your coordinate rounded to two decimals (about 1 km) to Kakao's local search. Nothing else about you is sent, and the results are not stored.

That is all. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking.

Location

Pawday asks for "While Using the App" location permission so it can sort places by distance, pick your region and fetch local weather. This is optional: tap "Don't Allow" and choose a region in Settings instead. Your precise location is used on the device only. The weather request goes to Apple's WeatherKit service; the nearby search gets the rounded coordinate described above; nothing is sent to our own servers (we have none).

Network

  • A static daily card per region (weather summary, picks, trending themes) from https://younatics.github.io/apps/pawday/today/. The request carries no identifier and no location.
  • Apple WeatherKit for local weather.
  • Kakao Local, Daum blog and image search for nearby places, review previews and photo previews (shown when you look, never stored).
  • Google Places API for a place's photos, rating, review count and hours. Place photos and ratings stay on your device for at most 30 days.
  • Apple CloudKit public database for the anonymous counters above.
  • "Ask Pawday" runs on the device (Apple Intelligence where available); your questions are not sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your dog's profile (name, size, breed), favorites, chosen region, radius and "what matters to me" chips are saved in a file inside the app's container on your iPhone. The widget reads a small daily card through the app group. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Place data

Place listings are bundled with the app and come from Korea's open data portal (data.go.kr), the Korea Culture Information Service Agency's nationwide pet-friendly facility file and the Korea Tourism Organization, published under open government licences. Every place shows its source and last-checked date. Photos credit their source (Korea Tourism Organization, Wikimedia Commons with licence, or Google Maps with the author).

Children's privacy

Pawday collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

개인정보 처리방침 (요약)

댕오늘에는 계정·광고·추적이 없어요. 수집하는 것은 둘뿐이에요. ① 장소를 열거나 즐겨찾기·👍👎 를 누르면 익명 집계(장소·종류·시각·화면·순서·대략의 설정 맥락, 식별자 없음)가 iCloud 공개 테이블에 더해져 "지금 뜨는 곳" 을 만들어요. 하루 한 번 추천이 즐겨찾기를 얼마나 맞히는지 기기 안에서 재고, 그 결과(비율 둘·숨긴 개수·대략의 설정 맥락·빌드 번호)만 같은 테이블에 보내요 — 장소도 식별자도 없어요. ② 근처 애견동반 카페·식당을 보여 주려고 카카오 검색에 1 km 단위로 뭉갠 좌표만 보내요. 정확한 위치는 기기 안에서 거리순 정렬·지역 선택·내 위치 날씨(Apple WeatherKit)에만 써요. 사진·평점·후기는 보는 순간 Google Maps 와 카카오에서 가져오고 장소 사진·평점은 30일까지만 기기에 둬요. 강아지 프로필·즐겨찾기·지역·반경은 iPhone 안의 앱 저장소에만 있고 앱을 지우면 함께 지워져요.
