Pawday (댕오늘) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Pawday is a map of dog-friendly places in Korea with a daily walk index. It is designed to be local and private.

Data we collect

None. Pawday does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

Location

Pawday asks for "While Using the App" location permission so it can sort places by distance and pick your region. This is optional: tap "Don't Allow" and choose a region in Settings instead. Location is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere or stored outside the app.

Network

The app's only network request downloads a small static file with the day's weather summary for your region from https://younatics.github.io/apps/pawday/today/. The request contains no identifiers and no coordinates. If the file is not available, the walk index is computed without weather.

Data stored on your device

Your dog's profile (name, size, breed), favorites, chosen region and recommendation radius are saved in a JSON file inside the app's container on your iPhone. The widget reads a small daily card mirrored through the app's own keychain access group. Removing the app removes this data.

Place data

Place listings are bundled with the app and come from Korea's open data portal (data.go.kr) and the Korea Culture Information Service Agency's nationwide pet-friendly facility file, published under open government licences. Each place shows its source and the date it was last checked. "Reviews" opens a web search in Safari; "Report a problem" opens the Mail app with a message you choose to send.

Children's privacy

Pawday collects no data from anyone, including children.

개인정보 처리방침 (요약)

댕오늘은 개인정보를 수집·전송·공유하지 않아요. 계정·광고·분석이 없어요. 위치 권한은 선택이며 기기 안에서 거리순 정렬과 지역 선택에만 써요. 네트워크는 지역별 날씨 요약 정적 파일을 받는 데만 쓰고 식별자와 좌표를 보내지 않아요. 강아지 프로필·즐겨찾기·지역·반경은 iPhone 안의 앱 저장소에만 있고, 앱을 지우면 함께 지워져요.
