Pawday (댕오늘) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Pawday is a map of dog-friendly places in Korea with a daily walk index. It has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it keeps your precise location on your device.

Data we collect

Anonymous activity counters. When you open a place, add or remove a favorite or vote 👍/👎, the app adds a count to a public iCloud (CloudKit) table: the place id, the kind of action, the time, which screen it was on and in which position, and your coarse settings context (dog size, transport, weekend, weather). No user id, no device id, no location, no text. These counts power "Trending now". When the app shows a list of places, it also sends to the same table a random number made for that one list (it is new for every list and is not tied to your device or to you), the order of the places in the list, and which of its cards were actually on screen. We use these to improve the recommendations and to measure how well they work. Once a day the app also measures, on the device, how well its picks match your favorites and sends only the result to the same table: two percentages, the number of hidden favorites, your coarse settings context and the build number. No place, no identifier. The app also sends one anonymous count the first time it opens each day, week and month, and once when onboarding is finished (plus a count when you open directions, a website or the report form), so we can see how many devices use Pawday, never which ones. Apple classifies this as "Product Interaction", not linked to you, used for the app's features and for analytics.

Coarse location for weather and search. To show the weather where you are, the app sends your coordinate rounded to two decimals (about 1 km) to Apple's WeatherKit. When you search for a name that is not in our list and your search names no area, Apple Maps search gets the same rounded coordinate as the centre of its search. Nothing else about you is sent.

That is all. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking.

Location

Pawday asks for "While Using the App" location permission so it can sort places by distance, pick your region and fetch local weather. This is optional: tap "Don't Allow" and choose a region in Settings instead. Your precise location is used on the device only. Apple's WeatherKit and Apple Maps search get only the rounded coordinate described above. Nothing is sent to our own servers (we have none).

Network

  • Daily files we sign (the place list, a card and a ten-day trip forecast per region, rankings, the list of owner photos) from Apple's CloudKit public database, and owner photos from https://younatics.github.io/apps/pawday/. The requests carry no identifier and no location.
  • Apple WeatherKit for local weather (the rounded coordinate above).
  • Apple Maps search for a name that is not in our list. It gets the words you typed and a search area: around the rounded coordinate above when your search names no area, around that area otherwise. Versions 1.6 and earlier sent the unrounded coordinate for a search that names no area; 1.7 rounds it.
  • Kakao's Daum blog and image search for review previews and bloggers' photos (shown when you look). The request carries the place's name and no location. When a place has no photo of its own, the link to one blogger's photo and its image stay on your device for at most a week, credited with the blog it came from. Kakao's answers (review previews and searches) are also kept on your device for up to a week, so the app does not ask again every time you open it.
  • Photos load from where they are hosted (the Korea Tourism Organization, a blog's server, Wikimedia Commons, our site for owner photos). Those servers see an ordinary image request from your device; nothing else is sent.
  • Apple CloudKit public database for the anonymous counters above.
  • "Ask Pawday" runs on the device (Apple Intelligence where available); your questions are not sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your dog's profile (name, size, breed), favorites, chosen region, radius and "what matters to me" chips are saved in a file inside the app's container on your iPhone. The widget reads a small daily card through the app group. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Place data

Place listings are bundled with the app and updated from the CloudKit public database. They come from Korea's open data portal (data.go.kr), the Korea Culture Information Service Agency's nationwide pet-friendly facility file and the Korea Tourism Organization, published under open government licences. Every place shows its source and last-checked date. Photos credit their source (Korea Tourism Organization, Wikimedia Commons with licence, the shop for photos its owner sent, or the blog a blogger's photo comes from).

Children's privacy

Pawday collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

댕오늘에는 계정·광고·추적이 없어요. 수집하는 것은 둘뿐이에요. ① 장소를 열거나 즐겨찾기를 하거나 풀거나 👍👎 를 누르면 익명 집계(장소·종류·시각·화면·순서·대략의 설정 맥락, 식별자 없음)가 iCloud 공개 테이블에 더해져 "지금 뜨는 곳" 을 만들어요. 장소 목록을 보여 줄 때는 그 목록에만 쓰는 무작위 번호도 같은 테이블에 보내요. 번호는 목록마다 새로 만들고, 기기나 사람과 이어지지 않아요. 목록에 놓인 장소의 순서와 화면에 실제로 보인 카드도 함께 보내요. 이 정보는 추천을 고치고 추천이 얼마나 잘 맞는지 재는 데 써요. 하루 한 번 추천이 즐겨찾기를 얼마나 맞히는지 기기 안에서 재고, 그 결과(비율 둘·숨긴 개수·대략의 설정 맥락·빌드 번호)만 같은 테이블에 보내요. 장소도 식별자도 없어요. 또 하루·한 주·한 달에 한 번씩 앱을 처음 열 때와 온보딩을 마쳤을 때(그리고 길찾기·웹사이트·제보를 열 때) 익명 개수 하나를 보내서 몇 대의 기기가 쓰는지만 세요. 어느 기기인지는 몰라요. ② 내 위치 날씨를 보여 주려고 Apple WeatherKit 에 1 km 단위로 뭉갠 좌표만 보내요. 지역 없이 검색한 이름이 목록에 없으면 Apple 지도 검색에 검색어와 같은 뭉갠 좌표를 보내요. 정확한 위치는 기기 안에서 거리순 정렬과 지역 선택에만 써요. 후기 미리보기와 블로그 사진은 보는 순간 카카오에서 가져오고, 매번 다시 묻지 않도록 카카오의 답(후기 미리보기·검색)과 블로그 사진은 기기 안에 최대 일주일 둬요. 사진은 올려 둔 곳(관광공사·블로그·위키미디어, 사장님 사진은 댕오늘 사이트)에서 바로 불러와요. 그 서버는 기기의 보통 이미지 요청만 받아요. 강아지 프로필·즐겨찾기·지역·반경은 iPhone 안의 앱 저장소에만 있고 앱을 지우면 함께 지워져요.
