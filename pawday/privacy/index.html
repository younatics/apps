Pawday (댕오늘) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Pawday is a map of dog-friendly places in Korea with a daily walk index. It has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it keeps your precise location on your device.

Data we collect

Anonymous activity counters. When you open a place, add a favorite or vote 👍/👎, the app adds a count to a public iCloud (CloudKit) table: the place id, the kind of action, the time, which screen it was on and in which position, and your coarse settings context (dog size, transport, weekend, weather). No user id, no device id, no location, no text. These counts power "Trending now". Apple classifies this as "Product Interaction", not linked to you.

Coarse location for nearby search and weather. To show dog-friendly cafés and restaurants around you and the weather where you are, the app sends your coordinate rounded to two decimals (about 1 km) to Kakao's local search and to Apple's WeatherKit. Nothing else about you is sent, and Kakao's results are not stored.

That is all. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking.

Location

Pawday asks for "While Using the App" location permission so it can sort places by distance, pick your region and fetch local weather. This is optional: tap "Don't Allow" and choose a region in Settings instead. Your precise location is used on the device only. Apple's WeatherKit and Kakao's nearby search get only the rounded coordinate described above; nothing is sent to our own servers (we have none).

Network

  • Static daily files from https://younatics.github.io/apps/pawday/ (a card per region, rankings, owner photos and links to shops' own Instagram photos). The request carries no identifier and no location.
  • Apple WeatherKit for local weather (the rounded coordinate above).
  • Kakao Local, Daum blog and image search for nearby places, review previews and bloggers' photos (shown when you look). When a place has no photo of its own, the link to one blogger's photo and its image stay on your device for at most a week, credited with the blog it came from. Kakao's answers (nearby places, review previews, searches) are also kept on your device for up to a week, so the app does not ask again every time you open it.
  • Photos load from where they are hosted (the Korea Tourism Organization, Instagram, a blog's server, Wikimedia Commons). Those servers see an ordinary image request from your device; nothing else is sent.
  • Apple CloudKit public database for the anonymous counters above.
  • "Ask Pawday" runs on the device (Apple Intelligence where available); your questions are not sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your dog's profile (name, size, breed), favorites, chosen region, radius and "what matters to me" chips are saved in a file inside the app's container on your iPhone. The widget reads a small daily card through the app group. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Place data

Place listings are bundled with the app and come from Korea's open data portal (data.go.kr), the Korea Culture Information Service Agency's nationwide pet-friendly facility file and the Korea Tourism Organization, published under open government licences. Every place shows its source and last-checked date. Photos credit their source (Korea Tourism Organization, Wikimedia Commons with licence, the shop's Instagram account, or the blog a blogger's photo comes from).

Children's privacy

Pawday collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
