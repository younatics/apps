Daynest

A shared memory timeline for the moments you make together.

Download on the App Store — free

What Daynest is

Daynest is a private timeline that two people write together. You save a memory with its photos, notes, place, music and the weather at that moment, and it appears on both phones through iCloud. It answers one question — what memories did we make together? — and nothing else.

Simple by design

Daynest is not a planner, task list, chat app, or social network. It keeps one quiet timeline of memories with dates, notes, places, emoji, and selected photos. "On This Day" brings back a week, a month and a year ago, and home screen widgets show how many days you have shared.

Private first

Memories are saved on your device first and sync through your own iCloud. Sharing uses Apple's native collaboration share sheet. Daynest has no custom accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who uses it

Couples keeping a shared diary, families collecting the small days, and friends who want a quiet place for photos that is not a social feed.
